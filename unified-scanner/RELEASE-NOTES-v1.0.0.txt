NSE UNIFIED SCANNER v1.0.0

STANDALONE
- Package: com.suhas.nseunifiedscanner
- Target device: Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra / Android 16
- Does not change Multyfi AutoBuy.

SCANNER
- Full Groww NSE CASH EQ instrument universe.
- 5-minute refresh aligned to completed candles.
- Top three ranked 30-minute opportunities.
- VWAP, RSI14, MACD 12/26/9 acceleration, ATR14, relative volume, one-hour momentum, prior-day pivot R1/R2, wick quality, live spread, turnover, order-book depth, circuit room, market cap, 52-week data and whole-universe breadth.
- No chasing: a one-click buy is blocked if live price has moved >0.20% above the recommendation entry.

LEARNING
- Local SQLite recommendation ledger.
- 30-minute replay of every distinct recommendation.
- Target-before-stop = SUCCESS; stop = FAIL; timeout = TIMEOUT; target+stop within the same five-minute replay candle = AMBIGUOUS/excluded.
- Rolling last-100 recommendation hit rate shown at top.
- Bounded logistic model plus adaptive qualification threshold.
- After at least 30 resolved samples, sub-80% performance tightens the score floor rather than forcing trades.
- Learning cannot modify the +0.50% net objective, liquidity gates, maximum 30-minute duration or protection requirements.

EXECUTION
- LIVE OFF by default.
- One active MIS position only.
- User taps BUY; app sizes against available MIS margin using Groww margin API and configurable capital use (default 98%).
- Actual filled quantity and average price are reconciled before protection.
- Actual target is charge-aware and includes a slippage reserve.
- Attempts CASH MIS OCO; safely falls back to SL-M + app target if OCO is unavailable/rejected.
- Durable PENDING_PROTECTION state survives process interruption. Recovery first restores protection or exits.
- Unprotected fill triggers emergency market exit.
- Hard exit at 30 minutes or 15:10 IST, whichever occurs first.
- Dedicated-IP exact-match, encrypted credentials, idempotent references, duplicate/one-position guards.

DATA SCOPE
- PE and news are not fabricated or scraped in this release. They are not exposed as stable fields by the Groww Trading API. V1 prioritizes live technical/liquidity/market data that can be validated and rate-limited safely.
